# Stripe Policy & Constraints

## Stripe Usage Policy
- Stripe is used only for platform service fees.
- This service is NOT a donation or money transfer platform.
- Payments are for digital service usage only.

## Payment Model
- One-time payment per invitation
- No subscriptions
- No marketplace or Connect usage in current phase

## Refund Policy
Payments are non-refundable once the invitation sharing feature is activated.

## Important Notes
- Avoid mentioning gift money, wedding money, or transfers to third parties.
- Stripe Live activation will happen only after MVP readiness.
